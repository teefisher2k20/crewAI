## 2025-05-15 - CLI "Next Steps" Guidance
**Learning:** Users often feel lost after a CLI tool creates a project structure without clear instructions on what to do next. Providing a styled "Next Steps" guide with immediate actions (cd, install, run) significantly improves the onboarding experience and makes the tool feel more professional and helpful.
**Action:** Always include a clear, styled guide after any project initialization command. Use visual containers like `rich.panel.Panel` to make the instructions stand out.
