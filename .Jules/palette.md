## 2025-05-15 - [Project Guidance Panel]
**Learning:** After a successful creation command in a CLI, users often experience a "what now?" moment. Providing a clear, visually distinct "Next Steps" panel with actionable commands (like `cd`, `install`, and `run`) significantly reduces friction and improves the initial onboarding experience.
**Action:** Always include a structured guidance panel using libraries like `rich` after any non-trivial project initialization or modification command.
