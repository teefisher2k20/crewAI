## 2025-05-15 - [Added "Next Steps" Panel to CLI]
**Learning:** Post-creation guidance in CLIs significantly reduces cognitive load and improves micro-UX by providing immediate, actionable commands. Using styled panels (like `rich.panel.Panel`) makes these instructions visually distinct and "delightful" compared to plain text.
**Action:** Always include a "Next Steps" summary after destructive or constructive CLI operations to guide the user toward their next goal.
