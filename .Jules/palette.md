## 2025-05-15 - [Standardized Post-Creation Guidance]
**Learning:** Users often feel "lost" after a CLI project creation. Providing a consistent "Next Steps" panel with immediate actionable commands (cd, install, run) significantly improves the onboarding experience and reduces cognitive load.
**Action:** Always include a styled "Next Steps" Rich panel after any CLI command that creates new project structures or adds components to existing ones.
